# Content or immutable-asset incident

Owner: curriculum publisher. Severity: page for solution/privacy leakage; warning for incorrect content or stale derived indexes.

Containment: retire/quarantine the bad content version with the governed rights/security transition. Public projections, new attempts and retrieval must recheck availability. Do not rewrite already-published packets, historical version pins or review decisions.

Evidence: record exact source checksum, content/problem/manifest/index versions, withdrawal reason and aggregate affected scope. Keep learner answers, drafts and reasoning out of incident notes.

Recovery: allow the prior still-eligible version to serve new discovery; reconcile stale indexes and validate exact six-language manifests before any successor publication. Restore immutable asset bytes only when hashes match the backup manifest. Human publication reviews remain separate from engineering rollback tests.

Communication: publisher owns curriculum updates; execution/AI operators verify adapter and retrieval compatibility, and privacy administrator handles any disclosure assessment.
